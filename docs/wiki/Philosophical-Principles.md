# Philosophical Principles

These are the founder's stated principles, taken from the public manifesto ([canon/MANIFESTO.md](../../canon/MANIFESTO.md)). They are philosophy and positioning. They are not research findings.

1. **Structure before strategy.** High performance can continue while the identity structure beneath it erodes; the work addresses the structure first.
2. **Insight is not enough.** Information without structural change is described as information, not as change.
3. **Return, not transformation.** The stated aim is a return to the self that existed before the performance began. The category tagline is "Not transformation. Return."
4. **Order matters.** The pathway is sequential ([Sovereign Return](Sovereign-Return.md)).
5. **Body and business share one architecture.** Regulation is treated as part of how a leader works.
6. **Sovereignty means internally authored.** Authority is generated by the person, not extracted from a position ([Sovereign Leadership](Sovereign-Leadership.md)).
7. **Progress is shown by observable behavior.** Permanence is not promised and no outcome is guaranteed.

## How to read them

Metaphors, spiritual language and ideas borrowed from physics or neuroscience in the wider material are interpretive. They are not offered as established mechanisms of personal or business change. The separation between evidence and interpretation is explained in [Research Foundations](Research-Foundations.md).
