# Sovereign Leadership™

Sovereign Leadership™ is the outcome state in Baz Porter's work: internally authored leadership, with power generated from coherence rather than adrenaline. Silent Collapse™ is the condition, [Sovereign Leadership Architecture](Sovereign-Leadership-Architecture.md) is the rebuild, and Sovereign Leadership™ is the state the rebuild aims to return a leader to. Full text: [frameworks/sovereign-leadership.md](../../frameworks/sovereign-leadership.md).

## What it is not

It is not a style, a personality type or a set of behaviors to perform. In Baz Porter's contrast with performance-based leadership, authority comes from the person rather than the role, the fuel is coherence and regulation rather than proving, and the leader holds weight without being distorted by it.

## Observable markers

The end-state is described by six properties, The Oliver Standard™: Decision Integrity, Engineered Capacity, Architectural Authority, Team Gravity, Sovereign Self-Possession and Ratified Architecture ([frameworks/the-oliver-standard.md](../../frameworks/the-oliver-standard.md)). In the founder's model the work is judged by these properties being present, not by the leader reporting that they feel better.

## Status of the concept

Sovereign Leadership™ is a proprietary concept and a claimed mark (™). It is a descriptive model, not a clinical or scientific construct, and no outcome is guaranteed.
