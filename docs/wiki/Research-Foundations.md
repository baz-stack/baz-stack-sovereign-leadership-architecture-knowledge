# Research Foundations

Sovereign Leadership Architecture™ draws on, and is often associated with, ideas from psychology, neuroscience, physics, philosophy and spiritual traditions. This page explains how those sources are used. The detailed register is [research/ledger.yaml](../../research/ledger.yaml) (readable table: [research/REFERENCES_INDEX.md](../../research/REFERENCES_INDEX.md)), explained in [research/README.md](../../research/README.md).

## How sources are classified

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| established_empirical | Replicated or well-documented finding |
| debated_hypothesis | Live scientific disagreement |
| theory | Formal theory or interpretation, not settled fact |
| philosophy | Philosophical position |
| spiritual | Spiritual or religious teaching, preserved and not judged as science |
| practitioner_method | Applied method with limited or mixed evidence |
| metaphor | Figurative framing |
| founder_synthesis | Original synthesis by the founder |
| unsupported | No adequate support found |

## Evidence and interpretation are kept apart

- Cited research is background. It does not validate the founder's models.
- Quantum physics, consciousness theories, energetic models and similar frameworks are not presented as established mechanisms of business or personal change. Where earlier text used quantum language, it was relabelled as metaphor or removed.
- Each ledger entry states what the source supports and what it does not establish.

## Limitations of this register

The register holds 51 entries. None has been verified against the original publication: the sources could not be fetched in the environment where the register was built, so each entry rests on search summaries or background knowledge. Every entry is marked *hold until the citation is verified against the original* for external marketing use. The four third-party research references listed in [canon/CREDIBILITY.md](../../canon/CREDIBILITY.md) (a breathwork meta-analysis, a burnout statistic and two further research themes) are likewise unchecked.

## Peer-reviewed literature

Peer-reviewed sources appear in the ledger with citation details marked verified or unverified. No peer-reviewed publication authored by Baz Porter is claimed. An ORCID iD does not imply one.
