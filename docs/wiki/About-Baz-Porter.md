# About Baz Porter

## Public biography

Baz Porter is the founder of Baz Porter LLC™ and The Prestige Architect™, and the creator of Sovereign Leadership Architecture™. The work is aimed at high-achieving founders, executives and leaders who perform at a high level while privately experiencing what Baz Porter calls Silent Collapse™. Baz Porter describes the role as architect rather than coach: the work is positioned as structural, not motivational.

The approved public biography is in [canon/STORY.md](../../canon/STORY.md). Personal narrative detail is held privately and is not part of the public record.
