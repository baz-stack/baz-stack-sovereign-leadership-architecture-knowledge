# Sovereign Return

The Sovereign Return Architecture™ is the six-stage pathway within Sovereign Leadership Architecture™, from Silent Collapse™ to sovereign leadership. It was adopted by the founder as part of Sovereign Leadership Architecture™ (decision DEC-020). Full text: [frameworks/sovereign-return-architecture.md](../../frameworks/sovereign-return-architecture.md).

| Stage | Name | Core transition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognition | From unexplained disconnection to seeing what is happening |
| 2 | Identification | From seeing symptoms to understanding underlying patterns |
| 3 | Dismantlement | From operating through inherited patterns to questioning and changing them |
| 4 | Recalibration | From old responses to deliberately practiced new responses |
| 5 | Embodiment | From consciously practicing change to applying it consistently in life and leadership |
| 6 | Sovereignty | From dependence on external validation to sustained self-directed leadership |

## What this page does and does not claim

- The pathway is Baz Porter's own model. It is not a separately verified scientific theory.
- The stages are sequential in the founder's model. The statements about what happens when a stage is skipped are the founder's observations, not research findings.
- Stage completion is shown by observable behavior and demonstrated capability. No outcome is guaranteed and permanence is not promised.
- The mapping of stages to specific offers is not ratified (DEC-043), so none is published here. Curriculum detail is not published.
- Evidence-of-completion criteria exist only as proposals pending ratification.
