## Hard rules
- Quantum entanglement is not evidence for human consciousness links, manifestation, healing or business outcomes. String theory is not evidence for coaching mechanisms.
- Spiritual and philosophical influences are preserved in their own category, not discarded and not presented as science.
- Quotations commonly attributed to Darwin ("most responsive to change") and Einstein ("insanity") are misattributed per the ledger (RB-06, RB-08); agents must not use them.
- The mapping from specific founder claims to these entries, with verdicts, is in the restricted claim audit.
