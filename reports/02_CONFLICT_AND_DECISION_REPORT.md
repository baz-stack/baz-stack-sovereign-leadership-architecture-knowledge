## Summaries

**CON-001 - Protected material held outside the public repository.** Protected material is held in a separate private repository and referenced here by identifier and hash only.

**CON-002 - RAMS composition - four-part versus five-pillar.** Rosetta v4.2 lists the RAMS components with 'Attitude & Authenticity' joined; the founder instruction of 2026-10-16, the live repo and the framework registry state five pillars (Results, Attitude, Authenticity, Mastery, Systems).

**CON-003 - Lisa / Victoria / Oliver archetype usage.** Rosetta and the ICA define Lisa as the entry buyer and Victoria as the destination archetype. Live repo files still use Victoria as the leader before (Victoria-to-Oliver Bridge) and Oliver as the after-state.

**CON-004 - Entry door - Silent Collapse Diagnostic versus Fast Future Freedom Blueprint.** Resolved 2026-10-16: the Fast Future Freedom Blueprint is the entry door and the Silent Collapse Diagnostic is retired. llms.txt and baz-porter.txt are still to be refreshed.

**CON-005 - The Becoming - payment structure and continuation.** Sources differ on how The Becoming is paid for, what happens after the initial term, and its delivery cadence.

**CON-006 - Coming Home - one offer versus three tiers.** Rosetta and the product spec describe one Coming Home offer and call higher tiers historical. The live repo still publishes three tiers at three price points.

**CON-007 - The Gravity Code - fee structure, delivery and prerequisites.** Fee structure (two products versus one product with payment options), delivery components and prerequisites differ between the repo, Rosetta and the 16 Oct product spec. Reopened 2026-10-16: the founder's 16 Oct portfolio master records a rule that conflicts with Rosetta on prerequisites.

**CON-008 - Single-session offer price record.** The governing master and the product spec disagree on whether a price is confirmed.

**CON-009 - Implementation cohort format.** Duration and format differ between the governing master and the product spec. Delivery assets are not yet built; not launched.

**CON-010 - Business Profit Accelerator - format and price record.** Format described variously as a weekend immersion, a three-day immersion and a 35-hour intensive; one internal draft shows a different price figure.

**CON-011 - A Million Dreams - name, inclusions and sibling course names.** Name variants (Community/Circle), what the membership includes, and the Manifestation course title differ across sources.

**CON-012 - Succession program naming, status and scope.** A rename and defined structure for a succession offer appear in the governing master; no product spec or legal scope was supplied and the live repo shows a different name and curriculum size. Not launched.

**CON-013 - Three-lane architecture and extra offers absent from Rosetta.** The live repo organises offers into three lanes and lists offers (e.g., an esoteric lane, a consulting container, several courses) that do not appear in Rosetta v4.2, which states the progression is not one mandatory ladder.

**CON-014 - Stale canon files cite Rosetta v4.0 and retired offers.** Several live canon files state Rosetta v4.0 as their source and describe superseded offers, entry doors and ICA content. They conflict with Rosetta v4.2 and the 2026-10-14 ICA.

**CON-015 - Scientific and quantum language in public framework files.** Public files state quantum and neurological mechanisms as fact. Rosetta, the ICA and the product specs prohibit implying that quantum physics proves outcomes. These statements need founder-approved rewording.

**CON-016 - Credential, endorsement and proof claims need verification.** Public files assert partnerships, endorsements, awards, audience sizes, spend totals and client outcomes. Rosetta requires a claim-level proof index with consent before public use; none was supplied.

**CON-017 - Trademark and registration language.** Live files label many marks 'Trademarked' or 'Registered'. Rosetta and the handoff say symbols show claimed branding, not verified registration.

**CON-018 - Institution naming lineage and pilot.** Institution and pilot naming in the governing master is not reflected in the live repo, which still refers to an earlier institution label. Neither is launched.

**CON-019 - Routing score band inconsistency.** Advisory routing from the entry diagnostic sends one band to a business product although the diagnostic is described as identity-focused; the product spec says completion never auto-routes. The founder-supplied page describes a conversation with no score (DEC-039).

**CON-020 - Unspecified catalog items.** Several catalog items in Rosetta have no product specification in the handoff.

**CON-021 - Policies present only in the retired offers file.** The 2026-10-01 Terms state the three-month minimum, 30 days notice and no refunds; the signed agreement templates state wire-only payment. The Terms themselves are silent on wire-only payment.

**CON-022 - RAMS wording differs inside the founder's own 16 Oct documents.** The Gate Home outline prints the four-phrase form of RAMS (Results; Attitude & Authenticity; Mastery; Systems) while the Green Room specification and DEC-002 use five pillars.

**CON-023 - Twelve Core Patterns: playbook versus repo module structure.** A Drive playbook names twelve patterns; the repo has six modules of two. The mapping is unknown.

**CON-024 - Naming lineage: Paradynamic, Paradigmatic, Dimensional Mastery, Master Ascension.** Several names for related esoteric-lane material exist across the repo and the Drive; one reconciled lineage is needed.

**CON-025 - Cosmic Laws: count, duplicates and wording provenance.** The repo says 74 laws; the deck reconciles to 74 only as 71 slides with one slide holding four laws, includes duplicates, and its wording provenance is unconfirmed.

**CON-026 - The Blue Book: author and rights unidentified.** A long teaching text is stored in the founder Drive under this name; its author is not named in the files read.

**CON-027 - Live site search snippets show retired tier naming and inconsistent RAMS wording.** Search-engine snippets of the live site (not page readback, so unverified) suggest the FAQ page still uses a retired tier name for the Gravity Code and that RAMS is expanded inconsistently across pages. The site pages could not be fetched from this environment.

**CON-028 - Terms and Privacy Notice: internal consistency points for counsel.** The Terms and Privacy Notice dated 2026-10-01 have points that need counsel review; see DEC-040.

**CON-029 - The Becoming agreement template differs from the operative Becoming terms.** The Becoming agreement template differs from the operative terms (founder confirmed the operative terms 2026-10-17); counsel to correct the template (DEC-041).

**CON-030 - Coming Home agreement template uses a retired variant name and labels.** The Coming Home agreement template is titled with a retired variant name and uses a retired tier label.

**CON-031 - Agreement templates and Terms still reference the retired Silent Collapse Diagnostic.** The Becoming agreement template makes the retired Silent Collapse Diagnostic the admission gate and lists the retired mark.

**CON-032 - Becoming template: accreditation credit and institution wording.** The Becoming agreement template refers to accreditation credit toward a forthcoming certification pathway at a named training centre, which the institution records do not define.

**CON-033 - Stage names differ between the Sovereign Return framework and the Coming Home agreement.** The Coming Home agreement template describes three stages with different names from the six Sovereign Return stages.
